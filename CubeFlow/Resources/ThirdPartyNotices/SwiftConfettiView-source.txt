Vendored UIKit emitter and four original particle assets; no package/runtime dependency.
CubeFlow adaptations: iOS compilation guard, namespaced resource lookup,
explicit synchronous cancellation for UIViewRepresentable teardown, and a
birth-stop/drain API that preserves existing particles until their natural end.
PB uses DefaultRainDemo intensity 0.75 / density 1.5 with a saturated five-color
multicolor palette. Emission lasts 3.2 seconds; the view then remains through
the maximum emitter-cell lifetime (10.5 seconds plus a 0.1-second margin).
No sound, haptic, alternate preset, or style selector. Default rain depth behavior retained.
