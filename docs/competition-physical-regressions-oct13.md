## Advancement Evidence and Changes

- CubeFlow's WCA Live calculation is server-side, not an existing local engine. Its flags remain authoritative and unchanged. Shared rank styling now centralizes its exact green **#00E676**, yellow **#FFF59D**, and black highlighted foreground for round lists and competitor cards.
- [WCA Live's advancement implementation](https://github.com/thewca/wca-live/blob/main/lib/wca_live/scoretaking/advancing.ex) establishes current qualification, complete/clinched qualification under optimistic missing attempts, tie-safe 75% limits, rank/percentage/strict-result rules, and actual next-round roster precedence. Final top-three successful results use its existing podium highlighting, not a fictitious subsequent round. - [CubingChina's public Live page](https://cubing.com/competition/Beijing-Autumn-Rivalry-2026/live) and its loaded results component confirm that its UI computes confirmed/projected highlights rather than simply coloring `advancementStatus`. - An opened next round causes one competition-scoped adjacent-roster request, independently of the displayed round. Evidence is validated against competition/round identities and revisions, reused, revoked on metadata/roster changes, and cancelled on selection/stop. Official published rows without advancement rules/participation evidence do not receive speculative yellow. China cards do not fetch every historical field merely to paint all ranks; only available, sufficient context is used. The mathematical checks include incomplete fields, cutoff failure, percentage/strict limits, tied boundaries, stale roster revocation and cancellation.

## Final-Audit Rounding Correction

- A last comparison with [WCA Live's attempt-result normalization](https://github.com/thewca/wca-live/blob/main/lib/wca_live/scoretaking/attempt_result.ex) found the over-ten-minute whole-second average rule missing from optimistic scoring. A deterministic mean of 700.1167 seconds rounds to 700 seconds and must tie a 700-second mean, with best breaking the tie. The old calculation incorrectly treated the first result as possibly qualifying rather than clinched.
- The new regression reproduced that exact failure (`rounding-before.log`). The calculation now applies whole-second rounding above ten minutes after normal mean rounding, while FMC points retain their separate scaling. The advancement-only retest passed **15 tests**, including the formerly failing case (`rounding-after.log`). The failed reproduction's xcodebuild process was stopped after its failed suite result when teardown lingered; it is not a passing run. The corrected run exited successfully.
- This correction occurred after the initial generic build. The user explicitly authorized one additional final build; that build passed against the corrected production source. No production edits followed it.
