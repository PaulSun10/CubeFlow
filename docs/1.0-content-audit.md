## Added Content And Permissions

### Square-1 PBL

Source: [PBL-Manager data](https://github.com/charlieharris0n/PBL-Manager/blob/18cf975e5e22c474be8788eab4e4cb469cf90c1b/public/assets/data/init.json), pinned commit `18cf975e5e22c474be8788eab4e4cb469cf90c1b`. [MIT license](https://github.com/charlieharris0n/PBL-Manager/blob/18cf975e5e22c474be8788eab4e4cb469cf90c1b/LICENSE) retained in `Resources/ThirdPartyNotices/PBL-Manager-MIT.txt`.

The source's algorithm arrays are empty. The imported data establishes setups, identifiers and top/bottom recognition groups, **not** curated algorithms. CubeFlow generates a labelled inverse-setup reference, reversing steps and negating signed layer rotations while retaining self-inverse slices. Every one of the 967 setups is slice-legal and its reference returns exactly to the initial piece state, including the middle flip. These references can be long and are not recommended competition fingertricks.

### FTO

Source: [Chen Shuang's standalone MIT FTO solver](https://gist.github.com/cs0x7f/1579492aa43c794d529057386c489702), revision `30ef16c7db1d2758fe359786ee140a0f321b94e9`. Its own inline license is the permission basis, not csTimer's parent GPL license. The notice is retained in the engine and in ThirdPartyNotices.

The 16 cases are the two directions of a three-edge cycle around each of eight faces, with all other piece orbits solved relative to the face palette. Setup/solution pairs are solver-generated and checked for exact inverse return. The solver may normalize a global face-color orientation; that is explicitly noted. Images use CubeFlow's independently unfolded net. These are **reference solutions**, not imported Bencisco/Nautilus algorithms.

The [Nautilus author's summary](https://nautilusfto.com/Summary/) and [last-layer guide](https://nautilusfto.com/Step4/) identify block/center/triple construction and L6X/L3C, OLL/PLL and one-look last-layer options. They are suitable future case-family research, not proof of permission to redistribute their complete algorithms/assets. No such data was bundled. Bencisco-specific training remains a separate, permission-checked content project.

## Square-1 OBL And Unresolved Taxonomy

[sq1obl.com](https://sq1obl.com/) describes OBL as combined corner/edge orientation, with a 74-case reduced tree. Mirror-expanded enumerations use different equivalence conventions; counts should not be mixed. No explicit dataset/image redistribution permission was established, so no bulk OBL import is shipped in this pass. Obtain permission and record equivalence, mirror and recognition metadata before importing.

Earlier research identified Daniel Karnaukh's 967-case PBL sheet, with Karnotation and no established redistribution permission. It was not imported or newly certified here. A future import requires author permission, an explicit notation converter and independent case validation. The requested Chinese labels for additional EP/PBL variants do not yet have an unambiguous authoritative definition: do not invent a category or relabel ordinary EP/PBL to satisfy a count.

Existing SpeedCubeDB-derived resources remain unchanged. Their attribution is not, by itself, a verified redistribution license. A release content-rights audit/permission record remains necessary; this pass does not retroactively certify legacy licensing.

## Diagram Root Cause And Fix

Several existing EP PNGs are already cropped in the file: enlarging a SwiftUI cell cannot restore missing pixels. The acquisition pipeline's fixed-height/overflow-hidden HTML container can cut rotated Square-1 geometry. Instead of re-downloading or guessing pixels, the image provider now renders complete top and bottom layers from the existing signed setup notation, before falling back to the original PNG.

The renderer tracks 12 sectors per layer, 30-degree edge / 60-degree corner shapes and legal slash boundaries. It reserves the full rotated corner radius, draws corner side colors and uses a transparent `224 x 456` point canvas. This fixes all 49 EP case diagrams and the other families with valid setups. The same full image reaches thumbnails and details; no per-case offset or parent-frame cropping patch is introduced.

Four pre-existing setups fail the chosen baseline's legal-slice check:

- `lin.json/cb`
- `sq1linparitypll.json/cb` (same underlying case)
- `sq1cs.json/muffin_right_paw`
- `sq1eo.json/4_4`

They keep their original assets. Their setup orientation/convention must be resolved against a reliable source before generating replacements; they are not silently changed into different cases. The existing solved CS no-op is intentionally valid.

Image caching uses `NSCache` with a 128-item / 32 MiB cost budget instead of retaining the entire expanded library indefinitely. The set Info toolbar now uses plain `info`, without redundant nested circles. No Alg UX restructure or new training flow is enabled.

## Validation Scope

`tools/ContentValidation/validate-library.cjs` checks all set metadata, case/algorithm IDs and basic field validity; validates existing Square-1 setup slice legality; checks every new PBL inverse and FTO setup/solution. It explicitly reports the four closed baseline exceptions and fails on a new exception. It is **not** a full semantic solver for all 6,603 legacy cube/Megaminx/Pyraminx algorithms.

`DepthPassTests` validates the native parsers, all imported cases, all 49 EP image bounds, and actual bundle availability. Independent FTO state checks and raster/compositing checks are described in the event audit and validation README.

Before release, manually check EP/PBL case recognition against a real Square-1, FTO color/face convention against a physical FTO, thumbnails/details at accessibility text sizes, notation wrapping, VoiceOver, light/dark appearance, and scrolling through the larger PBL family. Generated references are intentionally not a substitute for curated teaching content.
